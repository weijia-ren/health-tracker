# Product brief: Daily Health Check-in

## Product promise

Open it once a day. Tap a few check-ins. See your sleep, nutrition, exercise, and stress patterns over time. Your data stays private on your device.

## Target users

People who want a simple, non-judgmental way to notice health patterns without signing up for another account or sharing sensitive health data.

## Primary jobs

1. Track daily health progress quickly.
2. Learn practical health tips and resources.
3. Review weekly and 30-day patterns.
4. Keep data private and under user control.

## MVP scope

- Mobile-first daily check-in
- Four areas: sleep, nutrition, exercise, stress/recovery
- Today completion score
- Weekly and 30-day trend summaries
- Health resources section
- Local browser storage
- Export/import/delete data
- Privacy promise and health disclaimer
- PWA-ready structure

## Out of scope for first free beta

- User accounts
- Cloud sync
- Social sharing
- Push notifications
- Apple Health/Fitbit integration
- Personalized medical recommendations
- Paid subscription

## Success criteria for first beta

- A new user understands the tool in under 30 seconds.
- Daily check-in takes less than 2 minutes.
- Users understand that data stays on their device.
- Users can export a backup.
- Users find at least one health resource useful enough to revisit.
