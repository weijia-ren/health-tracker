# Privacy and safety notes

## Privacy model

The first public beta should be local-first:

- No account
- No backend database
- No server-side storage of health check-ins
- User data stored in browser localStorage
- User controls export, import, and delete

## Plain-language privacy copy

Your check-in data is stored only in your browser on your device. We do not collect, view, or store your health data. If you clear browser data or switch devices, your entries may be lost unless you export a backup.

## Health disclaimer copy

This tool is for personal reflection and habit tracking only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Talk with a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.

## Important product safety rule

Do not present resources as personalized medical advice. Keep tips general, practical, and source-linked.

## If cloud sync is added later

Before collecting user health data on a server, revisit:

- Privacy policy
- Data deletion rights
- Security controls
- Encryption
- Authentication
- Backups
- Compliance obligations
- Incident response
